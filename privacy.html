Out of Eden may use cookies, local storage, and similar technologies for website functionality, security, and Google advertising. Google and its partners may use cookies or other identifiers to deliver, measure, and, where permitted, personalize advertisements based on visits to this and other websites.

You can manage cookies through your browser settings. Where required by applicable law, we will request consent before using non-essential advertising technologies. Rejecting optional cookies will not prevent you from playing the game, although advertisements may be less relevant.

Out of Eden uses or plans to use Google advertising services, including Google AdSense, to display advertisements. When advertising is enabled, Google and its advertising partners may process information such as IP addresses, device and browser information, ad interactions, and cookie or similar identifiers to serve and measure ads, prevent fraud, and personalize ads where allowed.

For information about Google's use of data, visit How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. You can manage ad personalization through Google My Ad Center.

Advertising preferences and consent options may vary depending on your region. Where legally required, we will provide an appropriate consent mechanism before activating personalized advertising or storing non-essential identifiers.

6. Optional Facebook Sharing

The game may offer a button that lets you voluntarily share game-related content, such as a result or a link to Out of Eden, on Facebook. Sharing is optional and is not required to play.

When you choose to share, you may be redirected to Facebook or shown a Facebook sharing interface. Facebook may process information about your interaction, including information associated with your Facebook account, under Meta's Privacy Policy. You control whether to complete the post and its audience using Facebook's available settings.

Out of Eden does not automatically post to your Facebook account and does not receive your Facebook password or private messages through this sharing feature. A standard outbound sharing link does not require us to embed Facebook tracking tools on the game page.

7. Sharing of Information

We do not sell personal information submitted through the game. Technical information may be processed by infrastructure and advertising providers as described above, or disclosed where required by applicable law. Choosing to share on Facebook is a separate, voluntary action.

8. Children's Privacy

Advertising settings and consent measures appropriate to the audience and applicable law will be used when ads are enabled. If you are a parent or guardian with a privacy concern about a child's use of the website, please contact us at contacto@raulrodriguez.cl.

Depending on your location, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information. To make a request, contact contacto@raulrodriguez.cl.
